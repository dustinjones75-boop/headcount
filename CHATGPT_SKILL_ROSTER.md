## Active-core principle

The router should treat roughly 25–36 of these as frequently active depending on the user's current work. The remaining roster is available as supporting expertise. Do not load or invoke every specialist by default.

## Upstream relationship

Where an upstream Headcount skill exists, preserve its useful domain framework while adapting Claude-specific mechanics to ChatGPT's tool and connector model. Custom ChatGPT skills may extend the upstream roster when a recurring user workflow is not represented well upstream.
