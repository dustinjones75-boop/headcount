# Paid Advertising

Paid media should be judged by economics and downstream quality, not clicks alone.

## Invoke when
Use for campaign setup/restructure, keyword or audience strategy, ad creative, bidding/budget questions, performance diagnosis, scaling decisions, or channel comparisons.

## Diagnose in order
1. **Tracking** — verify that the intended conversion fires correctly end to end.
2. **Serving** — impressions, eligibility, budget limits, rank, geography, and schedule.
3. **Traffic quality** — search terms/audiences, match type, intent, exclusions, placements.
4. **Creative response** — CTR and message-market match.
5. **Landing-page conversion** — CVR, message continuity, friction, mobile experience.
6. **Economics** — CPL/CPA versus gross margin or expected commission/contribution.
7. **Downstream quality** — lead quality, booking/sale rate, retention when available.

## Structure
- Search: separate materially different intent tiers; do not let broad research traffic consume high-intent budget.
- Social: isolate meaningful audience/creative hypotheses without starving campaigns of data.
- Keep naming conventions consistent enough to support reporting and comparison.
- Avoid unnecessary fragmentation at low volume.

## Testing discipline
Test genuinely different hypotheses. Change one major variable at a time. Structural tests — intent, offer, page, form, and creative angle — outrank cosmetic tests.

## Tool behavior
If connected campaign or analytics data is available, inspect it before diagnosing. When the user provides screenshots, exports, landing-page URLs, or conversion definitions, treat them as evidence. Check current platform policies or features on the web when they could have changed.

Do not say tracking works merely because a conversion action exists; look for evidence of a test or observed conversions when possible.

## Collaboration
Use `landing-page-cro` when traffic appears qualified but conversion is weak; `marketing-analytics` when attribution or tracking is uncertain; `unit-economics` when deciding whether to scale; `behavioral-marketing` for offer/message issues.

## Return contract
Lead with the most likely constraint. Show the evidence. Separate confirmed problems from hypotheses. Recommend the smallest set of changes in priority order, plus a read-by date/volume threshold and a kill-or-scale rule.
