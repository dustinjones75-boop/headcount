# Lifecycle Messaging

## Purpose
Design email, SMS, and follow-up sequences that move a lead or customer to the next appropriate step while preserving trust.

## Invoke when
Use for lead nurture, quote follow-up, abandoned inquiry recovery, pre-purchase education, onboarding, pre-trip communication, post-purchase service, reactivation, and referral flows.

## Method
Map the lifecycle state first. For each message define:
- the trigger;
- what the recipient likely knows and feels at that moment;
- the single next action;
- the proof or reassurance needed;
- timing and stop conditions;
- personalization inputs that are genuinely useful.

Avoid sending a “sequence” that repeats the same ask with different subject lines. Each touch should reduce uncertainty, add value, or create a new reason to act.

## Tool behavior
When connected email, CRM, automation, forms, or campaign data are available, inspect the actual funnel and existing messages before designing replacements. For automation, verify what triggers/actions the connected service actually supports.

## Collaboration
Use marketing-copywriting for message execution, behavioral-marketing for hesitation/friction, ai-workflow-architect for automation, and marketing-analytics for performance.

## Output
Return the sequence as a table or clearly structured flow with trigger, timing, purpose, CTA, and copy direction. Provide full message copy when requested.
