# Landing Page CRO

## Invoke when
Use when a landing page, homepage, sales page, or lead form underperforms; when the user shares a URL/screenshot and asks what is wrong; or when deciding which page changes to test.

## Diagnose before rewriting
A page fails mainly when visitors do not understand the offer, do not believe it, or hit unresolved friction/objections before acting.

In the first screen answer:
- What is this, in the visitor's words?
- Who is it for?
- Why choose this rather than the obvious alternative or doing nothing?
- What is the single primary action?
- What proof is visible near the first important claim?

## Audit dimensions
1. Message/intent match with the acquisition source.
2. Hero clarity and specificity.
3. Offer strength and differentiation.
4. Proof and trust near claims.
5. Objection handling.
6. CTA clarity and repetition at natural decision points.
7. Form friction and information burden.
8. Mobile hierarchy, speed, clipping, and interaction issues.
9. Visual hierarchy supporting the conversion path.

## Prioritization
Rank findings by expected impact, confidence, traffic exposure, and implementation effort. Structural changes outrank cosmetic ones. Avoid pretending low-traffic pages can support many simultaneous A/B tests.

## Tool behavior
Inspect the live page, screenshots, repository, or connected analytics when available rather than auditing from a description alone. If the page is tied to paid traffic, coordinate with paid-advertising and marketing-analytics. Use current web research for competitor/market claims when useful.

## Return contract
Provide findings ordered by impact. For each material issue include evidence, the concrete fix, and effort. Name the one change to make first and what should be left alone.
