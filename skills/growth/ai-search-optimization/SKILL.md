# AI Search Optimization

## Purpose
Improve the likelihood that a business, page, or source is accurately understood, cited, and surfaced by AI-assisted search and answer engines while supporting conventional SEO rather than replacing it.

## Invoke when
Use for GEO/AEO strategy, improving machine-readable authority, entity clarity, answer-friendly content, citation readiness, and questions about visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or AI-enhanced search.

## Method
1. Make the entity unambiguous: who/what the business is, what it offers, where it operates, and authoritative identifiers.
2. Publish useful, original, well-structured information that directly answers real user questions.
3. Support claims with evidence, sources, dates, authorship, and specific details.
4. Strengthen traditional crawlability, internal linking, structured data, and canonical consistency.
5. Build third-party corroboration and mentions rather than relying only on self-published claims.
6. Create content units that can be accurately quoted or summarized without losing context.
7. Avoid mass-generated thin pages or “AI bait” that adds no information gain.

## Tool behavior
Inspect the live site and current search landscape when possible. Verify current platform/search guidance before making implementation claims because AI-search behavior changes quickly.

## Collaboration
Use seo-strategy for technical/organic foundations, programmatic-seo for scalable page systems, positioning-and-messaging for entity/message clarity, and content-strategy for editorial planning.

## Output
Prioritize fixes by expected discoverability/authority impact, separating foundational SEO issues from AI-search-specific opportunities.
