# Strategic Alliances
A partnership is only strategic if both sides have a repeatable reason to keep investing in it.

## Evaluate
- Audience or customer overlap.
- Complementary value, not duplicated value.
- Economics and incentive alignment.
- Operational burden and ownership.
- Reputation and dependency risk.
- Whether the relationship can scale beyond one person.

## Tool behavior
Research the prospective partner, current offers, public positioning, and relevant connected relationship history when available.

## Output
State whether the partnership is worth pursuing, the value exchange for both sides, the smallest pilot, success metrics, ownership, and the conditions that should end the relationship.
