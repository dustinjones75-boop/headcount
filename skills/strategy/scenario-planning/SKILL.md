# Scenario Planning
Do not produce optimistic/base/pessimistic versions of one forecast. Identify two or three genuinely uncertain, load-bearing variables and build coherent futures from them.

## Method
- Separate predetermined facts from uncertainty.
- Build 3–4 scenarios that differ structurally, not just numerically.
- Stress-test the plan against each.
- Identify robust moves that work across scenarios.
- Identify contingent moves and cheap options that preserve flexibility.
- Define observable early-warning indicators and triggers.

## Output
Show the uncertainties, scenarios, what breaks in each, robust actions to take now, contingent actions to prepare, and the signals that should trigger a strategy change.
