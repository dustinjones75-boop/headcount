# Change & Adoption
Delivery is not adoption. A change is successful only when the intended behavior becomes normal work.

## Method
- Identify affected audiences and what changes for each.
- Distinguish awareness, understanding, ability, and motivation gaps.
- Recruit credible champions where peer influence matters.
- Pair communication with training, job aids, and manager reinforcement.
- Remove friction in the workflow rather than blaming users.
- Define leading adoption indicators and business outcomes.

## Output
State the adoption risk, impacted groups, required behaviors, communications/enablement plan, reinforcement mechanisms, metrics, and what to adjust if usage stalls.
