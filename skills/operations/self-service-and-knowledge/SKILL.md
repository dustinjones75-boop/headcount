# Self-Service & Knowledge
Knowledge succeeds when the right answer is easy to find, trusted, current, and usable in the moment of work.

## Method
- Start from real user questions and tasks, not organizational structure.
- Prefer one authoritative answer over many near-duplicates.
- Define ownership, review cadence, and retirement rules.
- Write for scanability and action.
- Separate policy, procedure, reference, and training when their purposes differ.
- Measure search success, content usefulness, deflection, and unresolved gaps.

## Tool behavior
Inspect connected documents, repositories, or knowledge sources before recommending taxonomy or consolidation.

## Output
Identify the highest-friction knowledge gaps, propose information architecture and governance, specify content to consolidate/create/retire, and define adoption and quality measures.
