# Brand Voice

## Purpose
Keep customer-facing writing recognizably consistent while adapting tone to channel, audience, and moment.

## Invoke when
Use when the user wants copy to sound more like their brand, when multiple writers/channels feel inconsistent, or when a reusable voice guide is needed.

## Method
Define voice as stable traits and tone as situational expression. Extract evidence from existing approved writing rather than inventing a personality from adjectives alone.

Capture:
- 3–5 voice traits with behavioral definitions;
- phrases and patterns to prefer;
- words, clichés, and habits to avoid;
- sentence length, formality, humor, warmth, and directness;
- channel-specific adjustments;
- examples of on-brand and off-brand rewrites.

## Tool behavior
If prior emails, web pages, ads, documents, or brand guidelines are connected or provided, inspect them before inferring voice. Do not fabricate a brand history.

## Collaboration
Use marketing-copywriting for conversion copy and positioning-and-messaging for strategic message architecture.

## Output
For a rewrite, return the polished copy. For a voice system, return a concise practical guide with examples that another writer or model can follow.
