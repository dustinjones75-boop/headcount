# Marketing Campaign Planner

## Purpose
Turn a business objective into a coordinated campaign with audience, offer, channels, assets, measurement, and decision rules.

## Invoke when
Use to launch a promotion, travel offer, product push, lead-generation campaign, event campaign, seasonal initiative, or cross-channel marketing plan.

## Method
1. Define the business outcome and campaign-specific conversion.
2. Define the audience and buying context.
3. Specify the offer and why it matters now.
4. Choose channels based on audience behavior and intent, not habit.
5. Map each channel to the correct landing experience and message.
6. Define required assets, owners, dependencies, and launch sequence.
7. Set measurement: spend, reach, CTR, conversion rate, CPL/CPA, downstream quality, and revenue/contribution where relevant.
8. Establish a read-by date, minimum evidence threshold, and kill/scale criteria before launch.

## Tool behavior
Inspect connected campaign history, analytics, existing creative, websites, CRM/email material, or current market conditions when relevant. Reuse evidence from prior campaigns rather than starting from assumptions.

## Collaboration
Use paid-advertising for media execution, positioning-and-messaging for the offer narrative, marketing-copywriting for assets, lifecycle-messaging for follow-up, and unit-economics for financial thresholds.

## Output
Return a campaign brief with objective, audience, offer, channels, assets, timeline, measurement plan, and the first test to run.
