# Content Strategy

## Purpose
Build a content system tied to audience questions, business objectives, distribution, and measurable outcomes rather than an isolated posting calendar.

## Invoke when
Use for editorial planning, topic selection, content pillars, campaign content, repurposing, and deciding what content is worth producing.

## Method
Map audience, objective, journey stage, topic, proof, format, distribution, and next action. Prefer reusable content assets that can be repurposed across channels. Avoid volume goals without a business hypothesis.

## Tool behavior
Use connected analytics, search data, existing content, and current web research when available to identify proven demand and gaps.

## Output
Return priority themes, formats, distribution plan, cadence only where useful, and the metric that determines whether each theme deserves continued investment.
