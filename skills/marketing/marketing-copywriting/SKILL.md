# Marketing Copywriting

## Purpose
Write persuasive, specific marketing copy that moves a defined audience toward a defined action without sounding inflated, generic, or manipulative.

## Invoke when
Use for landing-page copy, ads, emails, offers, headlines, CTAs, benefit framing, campaign copy, and rewrites intended to improve response.

## Method
1. Identify audience, context, desired action, and the strongest credible value proposition.
2. Separate features from benefits and benefits from outcomes.
3. Lead with what matters to the reader, not what the business wants to say about itself.
4. Prefer concrete proof, specificity, and plain language over adjectives.
5. Match copy to funnel stage and traffic source.
6. Address the largest objection close to the claim it challenges.
7. Use urgency only when the underlying reason is real.
8. Keep one dominant CTA per decision point.

## Tool behavior
When copy depends on an existing site, campaign, product, offer, email thread, or source document, inspect that material first. If current pricing, promotions, policies, or product facts matter, verify them before writing.

## Collaboration
Use positioning-and-messaging for strategic differentiation, brand-voice for tone consistency, behavioral-marketing for persuasion mechanics, and landing-page-cro for page-level conversion structure.

## Output
Provide ready-to-use copy first. When useful, briefly explain the strategic changes and any claims that need verification.
