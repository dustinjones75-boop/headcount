# Social Post Craft

## Purpose
Turn a useful idea, offer, update, or story into platform-appropriate social content without losing the underlying message.

## Invoke when
Use for posts, captions, short threads, carousel copy, promotional social content, and rewrites for a specific platform.

## Method
Lead with the strongest audience-relevant hook, keep one primary idea, make claims concrete, preserve the requested voice, and use a call to action only when it naturally advances the goal. Adapt structure to the platform rather than cross-posting identical copy.

## Tool behavior
When performance data or current platform context is available, use it. Do not invent engagement trends or platform rules.

## Output
Provide publish-ready copy and, when useful, one alternate hook or angle—not a pile of near-duplicates.
