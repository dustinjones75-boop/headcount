# Revenue Operations
Revenue leaks when ownership and stage definitions are vague.

## Design
- Define the lifecycle from lead → qualified opportunity → proposal/quote → booked/won → delivered → repeat/referral.
- Give each stage an entry condition, exit condition, owner, and required next action.
- Standardize follow-up timing and escalation.
- Remove duplicate data entry before adding automation.
- Track conversion by stage and source, not just total leads.
- Use connected CRM/email/automation data when available before diagnosing the funnel.

## Output
Map the current funnel, identify the largest operational leak, propose the revised stages/handoffs, define automation opportunities, and specify the metrics that should be reviewed regularly.
