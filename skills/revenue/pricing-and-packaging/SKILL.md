# Pricing & Packaging
Price should reflect value and economics, not arbitrary discounting.

## Evaluate
- Target customer and willingness to pay.
- Alternatives, including doing nothing.
- What is included, excluded, and perceived as premium.
- Margin and acquisition economics.
- Whether an incentive changes behavior or merely gives away value.
- Complexity introduced by tiers or bundles.

## For travel and service businesses
Separate supplier price from advisor-added value, perks, credits, group/block advantages, and any advisor-funded incentive.

## Output
Recommend the offer structure, price/incentive logic, expected customer reaction, economics, risks, and the simplest test before broad rollout.
